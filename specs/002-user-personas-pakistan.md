# User Personas Pakistan

## Persona 1: Office Worker Fat Loss

Works 9 to 6, eats chai and biscuits, lunch from home or canteen, late family dinner, low activity, wants belly fat reduction without extreme dieting.

Acceptance criteria:

- Plan includes walking after meals.
- Plan includes chai sugar reduction strategy.
- Plan gives roti/rice portion guidance.

## Persona 2: Beginner Woman Training at Home

Prefers privacy, has limited equipment, may have family meal constraints, wants safe beginner strength and fat loss.

Acceptance criteria:

- Home plan uses bodyweight, walking, stairs, bands, dumbbells, or backpack.
- Copy is respectful and non-shaming.
- App can show medical review warning if pregnancy or relevant caution is selected.

## Persona 3: Vegetarian Student on Budget

Needs cheap protein and practical meal ideas around daal, chana, lobia, eggs if allowed, dahi, paneer, soy, and rice/roti.

Acceptance criteria:

- Vegetarian guidance includes local protein options.
- Plan does not assume expensive supplements.

## Persona 4: Gym Muscle Gain User

Has gym access, eats Pakistani family meals, wants muscle gain without dirty bulking.

Acceptance criteria:

- Plan increases calories and protein.
- Workout uses progressive strength sessions.
- Meal guidance suggests adding carbs around training.
