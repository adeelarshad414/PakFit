# Diabetes Medication Plan Safety

PakFit supports diabetes-medication users with conservative planning copy only. The app does not diagnose diabetes, prescribe medicines, adjust insulin or oral medicines, replace glucose monitoring, or replace clinician care.

## Source Categories

- ADA blood glucose and exercise guidance: https://diabetes.org/health-wellness/fitness/blood-glucose-and-exercise
- ADA hypoglycemia symptoms and 15-15 rule: https://diabetes.org/living-with-diabetes/hypoglycemia-low-blood-glucose/symptoms-treatment
- CDC low blood sugar hypoglycemia guidance: https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/about/low-blood-sugar-hypoglycemia.html
- CDC diabetes physical activity guidance: https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/living-with/physical-activity.html
- IDF-DAR Ramadan diabetes fasting guidance: https://www.idf.org/media/uploads/2022/12/IDF_DaR_Practical_Guidelines_2021_web.pdf

## Product Behavior

- Android and iOS detect `DIABETES_MEDICATION` or `.diabetesMedication` as a plan modifier.
- Fat-loss calorie deficits and muscle-gain calorie surpluses pause until diabetes-clinician review.
- Meal guidance emphasizes consistent roti, rice, fruit, dates, dessert, and sweet-drink carbohydrate portions with protein and fiber.
- Ramadan guidance requires clinician review of suhoor, iftar, hydration, medication timing, glucose checks, low-glucose symptoms, and when to break the fast.
- Workout guidance switches to a diabetes medication clinician-reviewed movement plan with conversational walking, optional post-meal easy walks, light strength basics, and low-glucose stop guidance.
- Plan focus includes `Diabetes medication safety review`.

## Claim Boundary

PakFit must not diagnose diabetes, treat diabetes, prescribe insulin, prescribe oral diabetes medicines, adjust medicine doses, provide personal glucose targets, guarantee fasting safety, claim to prevent hypoglycemia, claim to reverse diabetes, replace a diabetes educator, replace a dietitian, replace a pharmacist, or replace doctor care.

## Manual Review Boundary

Before public release, clinical reviewers should check diabetes medication wording, Ramadan fasting copy, low-glucose escalation language, local Pakistan emergency wording, and store-listing claims.
