# PakFit v0.47.0 Release Notes

PakFit version 0.47.0 build 47 adds non-diagnostic PCOS metabolic and reproductive screening support.

## Added

- Android and iOS clinical risk factors for irregular or missed periods, excess hair or persistent acne, and known PCOS.
- Android and iOS PCOS metabolic/reproductive screening insight with clinician-review guidance.
- Food/activity guidance for insulin resistance risk using Pakistani meal anchors.
- Safety copy warning users not to self-start hormones, metformin, fertility medicines, or supplements from app guidance.
- PCOS clinical safety gate, documentation, store copy, CI wiring, and release evidence reporting.

## Boundary

PCOS support is educational screening only. PakFit does not diagnose PCOS, prescribe treatment, interpret fertility status, or replace gynecology/endocrinology review.
