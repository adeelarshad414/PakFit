# PakFit v0.49.0 Release Notes

PakFit version 0.49.0 build 49 upgrades high blood pressure support from marker screening only to a plan-modifying safety state.

## Highlights

- Android and iOS High blood pressure medical caution.
- Lower-sodium Pakistani food guidance that avoids salty lassi, achar, papad, packaged nimco, salty chutneys, and very salty restaurant foods as daily staples.
- Ramadan hydration guidance that avoids salted lassi or salty electrolyte drinks unless a clinician recommends them.
- Moderate clinician-review movement plan with conversational intensity, breathing cues, warm-up/cool-down posture, and stop-warning symptoms.
- Android unit coverage plus iOS smoke/XCTest coverage for blood pressure plan safety.
- Blood pressure plan safety gate, documentation, store copy, CI wiring, and release evidence reporting.

## Boundary

Blood pressure support is educational planning only. PakFit does not diagnose hypertension, prescribe medicine, change BP medication, guarantee BP reduction, or replace clinician care.
