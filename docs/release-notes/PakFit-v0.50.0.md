# PakFit v0.50.0 Release Notes

PakFit version 0.50.0 build 50 upgrades kidney disease support from warning-only behavior to a plan-modifying renal safety state.

## Highlights

- Android and iOS kidney safety behavior that caps high-protein fat-loss or muscle-gain targets.
- Muscle-gain workout labels switch to Kidney clinician-reviewed Movement Plan when kidney disease caution is selected.
- Renal dietitian review copy for protein, sodium, potassium, phosphorus, fluids, kidney stage, dialysis status, medicines, and labs.
- Supplement and self-treatment boundary for high-protein diets, creatine, unreviewed herbal kidney products, detox drinks, and supplement stacks.
- Android unit coverage plus iOS smoke/XCTest coverage for kidney plan safety.
- Kidney plan safety gate, documentation, store copy, CI wiring, and release evidence reporting.

## Boundary

Kidney support is educational planning only. PakFit does not diagnose kidney disease, prescribe renal nutrition, set dialysis-stage limits, recommend supplements, improve kidney function, or replace clinician care.
