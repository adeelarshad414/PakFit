# PakFit Demo Video Script

Estimated duration: 4 minutes 40 seconds.

## Scene 1: Product Opening

Duration: 15 seconds

Visual: PakFit dashboard and plan preview.

Voiceover: "PakFit is a Pakistani health, fitness, workout, and nutrition coach built around desi meals, daily calorie tracking, safe movement, and local health-screening workflows."

Transition: Cut to setup.

## Scene 2: Office Worker Setup

Duration: 35 seconds

Visual: Setup controls for age, weight, goal, office routine, diet pattern, training place, and equipment.

Voiceover: "As an office worker, you can set a realistic fat-loss goal, choose your routine, keep chai in the plan, and build guidance around roti, rice, daal, sabzi, canteen meals, and family dinners."

Transition: Cut to plan.

## Scene 3: Plan And Safety Guidance

Duration: 45 seconds

Visual: Plan tab with safety warnings, calories, protein, meal guidance, workout schedule, and prayer-aware timing.

Voiceover: "PakFit turns the profile into practical targets and a plan. Safety warnings stay above the plan, while meal guidance and workouts adapt to Ramadan, budget routines, eating out, gym access, home equipment, and medical cautions."

Transition: Cut to tracker.

## Scene 4: Daily Food Tracker

Duration: 40 seconds

Visual: Tracker workflow with hourly meal entries, manual food item, online search, and food photo estimate.

Voiceover: "The tracker supports meal-level and hourly logging. Users can add Pakistani foods, desserts, drinks, and custom family recipes, then review intake, burn, net calories, and recent history."

Transition: Cut to health.

## Scene 5: Health Markers

Duration: 45 seconds

Visual: Health marker inputs for BMI, cholesterol, uric acid, fasting blood sugar, blood pressure, HbA1c, hemoglobin, and diabetes status.

Voiceover: "The health workflow keeps screening educational and non-diagnostic. PakFit helps users notice BMI, cholesterol, uric acid, blood sugar, HbA1c, hemoglobin, diabetes status, and blood pressure patterns that should be reviewed with a qualified clinician."

Transition: Cut to clinical insights.

## Scene 6: Clinical And Mental Wellness

Duration: 45 seconds

Visual: Clinical intelligence, PCOS screening, PHQ-9/GAD-7, and Pakistan crisis resources.

Voiceover: "Clinical insights stay local and cautious. The app includes diabetes, blood pressure, cardiovascular, vitamin D, anemia, PCOS, and mental wellness screeners, with crisis support and clear boundaries that the app does not diagnose or prescribe."

Transition: Cut to dashboard.

## Scene 7: Analysis Dashboard

Duration: 40 seconds

Visual: Charts, progress bars, trend insights, todos, weekly/monthly summaries, and history.

Voiceover: "The dashboard converts daily records into progress bars, charts, trends, todos, weekly and monthly summaries, and a newest-first history so users can see what is improving and what needs attention."

Transition: Cut to safety modes.

## Scene 8: Advanced Safety Modes

Duration: 40 seconds

Visual: Pregnancy, blood pressure, kidney disease, diabetes medication, and Ramadan safety plan examples.

Voiceover: "For higher-risk workflows, PakFit modifies the plan. Pregnancy pauses weight-loss deficits, blood pressure mode lowers sodium guidance, kidney mode caps high-protein targets, and diabetes medication mode pauses aggressive calorie targets and keeps Ramadan timing clinician-reviewed."

Transition: Closing card.

## Scene 9: Closing

Duration: 15 seconds

Visual: PakFit app icon, store preview screenshots, and release artifact names.

Voiceover: "PakFit is local-first, privacy-aware, culturally familiar, and built with release gates for Android and iOS. It is ready for clinical copy review, device QA, and the next production distribution steps."

Transition: Fade out.
