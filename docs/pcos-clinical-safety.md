# PakFit PCOS Clinical Safety

PakFit now includes PCOS metabolic and reproductive screening support for Pakistani users in the Android and iOS clinical insights workflow.

## Current Scope

- Risk factor inputs for irregular or missed periods.
- Risk factor inputs for excess hair or persistent acne.
- Risk factor input for known PCOS.
- Screening score using PCOS review signs, South Asian BMI context, HbA1c, fasting blood sugar, and diabetes status.
- Clinician-review guidance for cycle changes, excess facial/body hair, persistent acne, fertility concerns, and glucose changes.
- Pakistani food/activity support focused on protein, daal or chana, sabzi, high-fiber roti/rice portions, and post-meal walking.
- Explicit warning not to self-start hormones, metformin, fertility medicines, or supplements from app guidance.

## Source Categories

- NICHD PCOS symptom guidance: https://www.nichd.nih.gov/health/topics/pcos/conditioninfo/symptoms
- CDC PCOS and diabetes risk guidance: https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/risk-factors/pcos-polycystic-ovary-syndrome.html

## Product Rule

PCOS copy must say screening, risk, review, or follow-up. It must not make diagnosis, treatment, prescribing, fertility-outcome, cure, or clinician-replacement claims.

## Release Boundary

This static gate proves source-level PCOS screening posture. Final production release still needs clinical copy review, device QA, legal/privacy review, and store age-suitability review before public distribution.
