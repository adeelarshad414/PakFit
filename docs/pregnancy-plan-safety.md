# PakFit Pregnancy Plan Safety

PakFit now treats pregnancy as a plan-modifying safety state on Android and iOS.

## Current Scope

- Pregnancy medical caution still creates a structured medical-review warning.
- Pregnancy pauses ordinary fat-loss calorie deficits and muscle-gain surplus logic.
- Pregnancy uses conservative target logic and asks users to review nutrition targets with an obstetric clinician.
- Pregnancy meal guidance emphasizes steady meals, protein, daal or chana, sabzi, fruit, dairy when suitable, safe hydration, and prenatal nutrition discussion.
- Pregnancy workout guidance switches to clinician-cleared gentle movement.
- Pregnancy movement guidance names stop-warning symptoms such as bleeding, dizziness, chest pain, calf swelling, painful contractions, or fluid leakage.
- Pregnancy plan focus includes `Pregnancy safety review`.

## Source Categories

- ACOG exercise during pregnancy: https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/exercise-during-pregnancy
- ACOG healthy eating during pregnancy: https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/healthy-eating-during-pregnancy

## Product Rule

Pregnancy copy must say clinician-reviewed, obstetric clinician, safety review, or stop-warning guidance. It must avoid prescribing, diagnosis, complication-management, universal safety, guarantee, or obstetric-care replacement claims.

## Release Boundary

This gate proves source-level pregnancy safety posture. Final production release still needs obstetric/clinical copy review, device QA, legal/privacy review, and store age-suitability review before public distribution.
